# CRM Business Insights

## Executive Snapshot

The analyzed CRM dataset contains 5,000 customer/lead records. The main performance indicators are:

- **Won Leads:** 819
- **Lost Leads:** 433
- **Conversion Rate:** 16.4%
- **Total Revenue:** $1,643,197
- **Average Revenue — Won Leads:** $1,448.83
- **Average Lead Score:** 59.4
- **Average Response Time:** 11.2 hours
- **Average Customer Satisfaction:** 7.37 / 10

## Key Analytical Themes

### 1. Funnel Performance

The lead funnel shows a meaningful gap between the total lead population and converted opportunities. Conversion rate should therefore be monitored alongside lead quality, response time, source, campaign, and sales-representative performance rather than treated as a standalone KPI.

### 2. Lead Quality

Lead-score analysis is incorporated into the dashboard to evaluate how lead quality relates to conversion. Higher-quality lead bands can be prioritized for faster follow-up and more focused sales effort.

### 3. Revenue Performance

Won opportunities generate substantially more revenue per won lead than the overall average across the full population. This makes won-lead revenue and revenue per won lead important metrics for evaluating sales effectiveness.

### 4. Customer Experience

Customer satisfaction and response time are included as operational KPIs. Monitoring both together helps identify whether service responsiveness may be affecting customer experience.

## Business Actions

- Prioritize high-score leads and define service-level targets for follow-up.
- Compare conversion and revenue by campaign, marketing source, segment, and sales representative.
- Monitor lost-lead concentration to identify funnel stages or sources requiring intervention.
- Track response-time and satisfaction trends together to identify customer-experience risks.
- Use the Power BI dashboard for ongoing KPI monitoring and drill-down analysis.
