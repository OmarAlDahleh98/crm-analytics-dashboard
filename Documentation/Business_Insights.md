# CRM Business Insights

## Executive Snapshot

The analyzed CRM dataset contains 5,000 customer/lead records. The main performance indicators are:

- **Won Leads:** 819
- **Lost Leads:** 433
- **Conversion Rate:** 16.4%
- **Total Revenue:** $1,643,197
- **Average Revenue — Won Leads:** $1,448.83
- **Average Lead Score:** 59.4
- **Average Response Time:** 11.2 hours
- **Average Customer Satisfaction:** 7.37 / 10

> **Scope note:** These figures represent the dataset-wide analytical snapshot. Power BI visuals are filterable, so displayed values can change with the selected reporting period and slicers.

## Key Analytical Findings

### 1. Funnel Performance

The dataset contains **5,000 leads**, with **819 won** and **433 lost** opportunities, resulting in an overall conversion rate of **16.4%**. This indicates that the largest opportunity is not simply increasing lead volume, but improving progression through the funnel and reducing avoidable losses.

### 2. Revenue Performance

Total recorded revenue is **$1.64M**, while the average revenue generated by a won lead is **$1,448.83**. This makes won-lead value an important complement to conversion rate when evaluating sales effectiveness.

### 3. Lead Quality

The average lead score is **59.4 / 100**. Lead-score analysis is incorporated into the dashboard to support prioritization of higher-quality opportunities and more focused sales follow-up.

### 4. Customer Experience

Average response time is **11.2 hours**, while average customer satisfaction is **7.37 / 10**. Monitoring responsiveness alongside satisfaction can help identify service-quality risks and opportunities to improve the customer experience.

## Business Actions

- Prioritize higher-quality leads and define clear follow-up service-level targets.
- Compare conversion and revenue by campaign, marketing source, segment, and sales representative.
- Analyze lost-lead concentration to identify funnel stages or sources requiring intervention.
- Monitor response-time and satisfaction trends together to identify customer-experience risks.
- Use the Power BI dashboard for KPI monitoring, filtering, and drill-down analysis.

## Analytical Limitation

The findings are descriptive and based on the available CRM dataset. Relationships between lead score, response time, satisfaction, conversion, and revenue should be treated as analytical associations rather than proof of causation.
